Frequently Asked

Common questions, plain answers.

What if I want changes to my site?

Included. Email us what you need and we'll take care of it. There's no extra charge for content updates, new sections, or design tweaks.

Do I need to do anything?

Almost nothing. We handle setup, hosting, configuration, and ongoing maintenance. The only thing we need from you is access to update your domain's DNS so we can point it at the new site. It takes one minute and we walk you through it.

What about my domain?

We'll set up your new site on your existing domain. If you need a new one, we can help with that too.

What if I want to cancel?

No worries. Your site stays live until the end of your current billing period. No penalties, no clawback, no awkward conversations.

When do I pay and how often?

Your first $29 is charged at signup via Stripe, then on the same day each month after that. Cancel anytime to stop future charges.

Who owns the content?

You do, always. If you ever leave, we'll hand over your content so you're never stuck.
